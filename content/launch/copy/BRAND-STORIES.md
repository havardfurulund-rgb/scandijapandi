# Brand roles (Havard, 13 Oct)
- Scandi Japandi Collection: a curated platform for design craft from Scandinavia/the Nordics. NOT a producer: never "our wool / we make / from our farm". Presents makers such as Dyra på Gården and Skredderen fra Damaskus.
- Stammehandverk: a collective for design and production of wool products, natural-fibre clothing, made-to-measure tailoring and art craft (kunsthåndverk). "We make" OK. Norwegian, like the site.
- Kita no Te – Hands of the North: a magazine (reportage, interviews, Nordic design trends) for Japan. Editorial, not selling.
- HIERARCHY: Stammehandverk (collective) → brands Dyra på Gården (wool) and Skredderen fra Damaskus (made-to-measure tailoring). ## Dyra på Gården: "The animals on the farm"
Logo: /workspace/scandi-makers/logo/final/dyra-logo-{dark,light}.svg (sheep / wool cloud)
On a small farm in Norway, the wool starts with the animals: alpacas, mohair goats and angora rabbits. They live outdoors in the grass and in the straw of the barn. Their fleece is carded and spun by hand on the farm, at a wooden spinning wheel, then woven on an old loom or knitted into pieces that are made one at a time.
Nothing here is mass-produced. Each sweater, scarf and hat is the only one of its kind, and the colour is often just the colour the animal grew.

## Skredderen fra Damaskus: "The Tailor from Damascus"
Logo: /workspace/scandi-makers/logo/final/skredderen-logo-{dark,light}.svg (needle / jasmine)
The Tailor from Damascus works in Norway. Every garment is made to measure (skreddersøm etter mål): cut for one body, sewn by hand, never off the rack. The jasmine in the mark is Damascus's flower; the needle is the work.
